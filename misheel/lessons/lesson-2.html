Exercise 6 · the last one you have not met

15 min · 15 marks

After today you will have met all six exercises on Paper 1. This is the other big writing task: an article giving your views, with four short comments from classmates printed next to the question.

Exercise 5 · the email

  • To one friend, informal
  • Three bullet points you must cover
  • Personal — what happened to you
  • Greeting and sign-off expected

Exercise 6 · the article

  • For a magazine or website, semi-formal
  • No bullets — comments instead, and you choose which to use
  • Opinion — what you think, and why
  • A title helps; no greeting, no sign-off
Where the marks are won

The comments are a starting point, not a checklist. Examiners give credit for using some of them and adding an idea nobody on the page has had. An article that only recycles the four comments stays in the middle band however well it is written.
