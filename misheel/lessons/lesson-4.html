One article, written by somebody who got full marks

10 minutes

The question was what makes a film enjoyable, and these were the four comments printed with it.

I like films about real events.
Too many films are about the same thing!
I prefer films with famous actors in them.
Most films are too long!

What makes a film enjoyable?

What makes a film worth two hours of your life? My class could not agree on a single thing, which is probably the most honest answer anyone gave. For me, though, one quality matters more than all the others.

Several people said they prefer films with famous actors, and others complained that most films are simply too long. I understand both. A familiar face is reassuring, and nobody enjoys checking their watch in the dark.

However, I think a film is enjoyable when I cannot guess what happens next. The actors can be unknown and the film can run for three hours; if the story keeps moving somewhere I did not expect, I never notice the time. My favourite film last year had nobody famous in it at all.

So a good film is not about length or stars. It is about whether you forget, for a while, that you are watching one.

Read it twice. First straight through. Then again slowly, with one question in your head: why is it in this order?

Five questions about that article

30 minutes

Answer each one out loud before you open it. Being wrong here costs nothing and teaches more than being told.

1Each paragraph is doing a different job. Say what each one does, in your own words.
1 · OpenNames the question, then says a position is coming. One quality matters more than all the others is a promise.
2 · Other sideTakes two opinions it does not share and admits what is true in them.
3 · Your sideThe writer's own answer, with a reason and an example.
4 · VerdictAnswers the question. Short, and does not introduce anything new.
2One word turns the whole article round. Find it.

However, at the start of paragraph 3. Everything before it is other people's view; everything after it is the writer's. It is the most useful word on the page and it costs one word.

3Which idea in the article is on none of the four cards? Point at the sentence.

"I think a film is enjoyable when I cannot guess what happens next." Nobody mentioned surprise. That sentence is the one earning the content marks — everything else could have been assembled from the cards.

4How many of the four cards are actually used, and are they quoted word for word?

Two of the four, and neither is quoted. I prefer films with famous actors becomes they prefer films with famous actors inside a longer sentence; Most films are too long! becomes most films are simply too long. The cards about real events and about films being the same were not used at all.

So you do not need all four. Two, reworded, is enough.
