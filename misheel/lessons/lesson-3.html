The rule that decides your content mark

The four comments are a springboard, not a list. If you only repeat them, you cannot go above 4 out of 6. The task says it out loud: you should also use some ideas of your own. At least one idea in your article must be yours.

Four paragraphs, four jobs

1 · Open — say what the question is and where you stand. Two sentences. No story, no "Hello everyone".
2 · The other side — take one or two comments you do not agree with and admit what is true in them.
3 · Your side — your strongest reason, plus the idea that is yours and not on the comment list.
4 · Verdict — one or two sentences that actually answer the question. Not "it depends".

Roughly 35 words each. That is 140, which lands inside 120 to 160 without you having to count.

Four days a week

25 minutes · 0511/11, June 2025
In class, you have been discussing whether employees should be required to work for a maximum of four days a week, giving them three days of free time.

Now write an article for your school magazine, giving your views.

Here are the comments from your classmates:

People would be happier!
Would they earn less?
Everyone would get more done.
The company would need more staff.

Before I show you anything — which two of those four would you put on your side, and which two would you have to answer?

Open
Should people work only four days a week? My class could not agree, and nor could I at first. After thinking about it, I believe a four-day week would be good for most people, but only if it is organised carefully.

What it does: names the question in the first line, and puts my position in the second. The words but only if buy me the whole of paragraph 2.

The other side
Some of my classmates worried that people would earn less, and that companies would have to hire more staff. These are fair points. A shop or a hospital cannot simply close on Fridays, and somebody has to pay for the extra workers.

What it does: picks up two comments without quoting them word for word — worried that and These are fair points are the handshake. Then it makes them concrete with a shop and a hospital.

Your side
However, I think tired people work badly. My uncle drives a bus six days a week and he admits he makes mistakes by Saturday. A rested driver is a safer driver, and that matters more than the number of hours.

What it does: However turns the article round — it is the most valuable word on the page. The uncle is my own idea. Nobody in the comments mentioned safety, and that is exactly why it earns the content mark.

Verdict
So I would support a four-day week, as long as nobody loses pay for it. We should judge work by what gets finished, not by how long someone sits at a desk.

What it does: answers the question with I would support, and the last line is a sentence worth remembering. 139 words in total.

Now half of one

20 minutes · 0511/11, Nov 2024
Your class has recently been talking about films, and you have decided to write an article for your school magazine. In your article, write about what you think makes a film enjoyable.
I like films about real events.
Too many films are about the same thing!
I prefer films with famous actors in them.
Most films are too long!

I have written paragraphs 1 and 2. You say paragraphs 3 and 4 out loud — the whole sentences, not the idea.

1 · OpenWhat makes a film worth two hours of your life? My classmates disagreed completely, which is probably the most honest answer. For me, one thing matters more than all the others.
2 · Other sideSeveral people in my class said they prefer films with famous actors, and others complained that most films are far too long. I understand both. A familiar face is comforting, and nobody enjoys looking at their watch.
3 · Your sideHowever, I think … your strongest reason, plus one idea that is not on a card
4 · VerdictSo … answer the question in one or two sentences
Before you speak

Paragraph 1 says one thing matters more than all the others. Paragraph 3 has to say what that one thing is. If it does not, the article does not hold together — and the examiner reads for exactly that.

only after you have said yours

3 — However, I think a film is enjoyable when I cannot guess what happens next. The actors can be unknown and the film can be three hours long; if the story surprises me, I do not notice. My favourite film last year had nobody famous in it at all.

4 — So a good film is not about length or stars. It is about whether you forget you are watching one.
