The whole rule

1

One thing: the verb takes an s. The film does. My teacher wants. The story is.

2

More than one thing: no s. The films do. My teachers want. The characters are.

3

It feels backwards, and that is why it is worth saying aloud. The s moves: the film works / the films work. One of them has the s on the noun, the other on the verb. Never both.

The one that catches everybody

When words come between the subject and the verb, your ear follows the nearest noun instead of the real one. The characters in the film … were, not was. Cover everything between and the answer appears.
